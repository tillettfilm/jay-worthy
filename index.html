How we work.

Time
I reserve ten working days each quarter, forty across the year.
Each Quarter
At the start of each quarter we agree how to spend the days across research, planning, travel, filming, footage review and editing. The balance can change as the story develops.
Visibility
I keep a shared record: dates worked, what was done, days used and days remaining.
Carry-over
Unused days in a quarter carry into later quarters.
Beyond Forty Days
Any further work is discussed and agreed before it's booked.
